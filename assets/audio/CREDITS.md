# Audio credits

Most sounds in this folder are procedurally generated by
`tools/synth.py` (no third-party content).

The three music loops are sourced from **OpenGameArt.org** under the
**CC0 Public Domain** license (no attribution legally required — listed
here as a good-citizen courtesy).

| File             | Original title                       | Author       | Source                                                                       |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `menu_loop.mp3`  | Happy Adventure (Loop)               | TinyWorlds   | https://opengameart.org/content/happy-adventure-loop                          |
| `loop.mp3`       | 8-bit Epic Space Shooter Music       | HydroGene    | https://opengameart.org/content/8-bit-epic-space-shooter-music                |

Both tracks were re-encoded to MP3 128 kbps stereo at 44.1 kHz for
consistent loudness and web-friendly file sizes. `loop.mp3` is shared
between Battle Royale and solo gameplay so the in-game music stays
consistent across modes.
