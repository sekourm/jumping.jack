## SFX

The remaining `.mp3` files (jump, land, bounce, bouncy, death, click,
pickup_*, platform_explode) are short procedurally-generated sounds. Replace
them with anything you prefer — the filenames are referenced by
`AudioManager` in `lib/services/audio_manager.dart`.
